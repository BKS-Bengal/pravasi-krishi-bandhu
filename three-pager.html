What it is
The Krishi Ratna League — a championship for farms, run in the format that made cricket unmissable. Across West Bengal's 294 constituencies, 5,000 young women and men each build a Smart Integrated Farm, mentored and graded through one season: Durga Puja 2026 to Durga Puja 2027.
Your part
You stand behind one of them — one named young person, in a district you choose. You are shown three applicants and you pick who you back. You are not buying anything and this is not a donation to a general fund.
The amount
₹1,00,000 for the season, as five monthly instalments of ₹20,000. ₹90,000 goes to the farm. ₹10,000 — ten per cent — runs the League: verification, mentoring, the platform, the prize pool.
What you see
A named farm and farmer. A dashboard with the activity log, photographs and the expense ledger. Progress against every instalment. The farmer's own video messages. An invitation to the Season 1 awards at Durga Puja 2027.

Who stands behind it

Seven decades, and eighteen years

Bharatiya Krishak Samaj

Founded 3 April 1955 by Dr Panjabrao S. Deshmukh, independent India's first Union Agriculture Minister — who kept it deliberately outside party politics so it could argue with any government of the day.

National President Shri Krishan Bir Chaudhary sits on the Government of India's Minimum Support Price Committee. Former Chairman of the State Farms Corporation of India and the Indian Sugarcane Development Council; founder of SFAC; former director of NAFED.

Advisory board: Dr R.S. Paroda (ex-DG, ICAR), Siraj Hussain IAS (ex-Secretary, Ministry of Agriculture), Ashish Bahuguna IAS (ex-Chairman, FSSAI), Dr Baldev Singh Dhillon (ex-VC, PAU), Gopa Sabharwal (ex-VC, Nalanda).

KarmYog for the 21st Century

Founded 2008 by Mahacharya Sourabh J. Sarkar — B.Tech, IIT Kharagpur; M.A. Education & New Media, Syracuse. Its subject has always been the same one: how a population changes a habit.

Kolkata Jaago — Safe Drive Save Life: 300+ schools and roughly one lakh taxi drivers. Jeevan-Jeevika Abhiyaan: changed farming practice in Odisha through the SRI technique.

OmniDEL: NSDC Best Learning Methodology Award 2014; a million-dollar TATA Trusts grant 2015. Now at the forefront of Agentic AI for informal-sector transformation — the capability this League's verification layer runs on.

KarmYog Green Village: ~12 acres of HIDCO wasteland turned into a working eco-village with 2,000+ fruit-bearing trees.

How a farm is graded

Satya (Truth) 30% · Mangal (Goodness) 25% · Sundar (Beauty) 20% · Samriddhi (Prosperity) 25%. A farm must be 100% chemical and pesticide free to be scored at all. A jury grades the farms; AI-assisted monitoring supports the farmer and records progress through the year. Prizes: ₹1 crore best farm, ₹1 crore best regional team, ₹1 crore across categories.

What we do not promise

This is a real farm, run by a real young person, against weather and pests and markets and their own inexperience. The farmer will do his best and so will we. We cannot guarantee success — no one honestly can. What we do guarantee is that a struggling farm will not quietly disappear from your dashboard. You will see the bad month, and what was tried.

The journey

2028

Supporters holding foreign passports or OCI cards — please read

KarmYog for the 21st Century Foundation holds 80G but does not yet hold FCRA registration, which Indian law requires before an organisation may receive a contribution from a foreign source — and under FCRA, an OCI cardholder or foreign passport holder is a foreign source regardless of where they were born.

So at this moment the invitation is to resident Indians and to Non-Resident Indians holding Indian passports. If you hold a foreign passport or an OCI card, please register your interest — FCRA registration is being pursued. We would rather tell you now than take your money incorrectly.
