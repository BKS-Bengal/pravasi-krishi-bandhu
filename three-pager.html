How you choose your farm

Tell us your bheete (ভিটে) — not just the district. The village, block, post office and police station of your ancestral home. We find applicants as close to it as we can, send you three, and you pick one.

What you receive, every month

  • A named farm and the farmer's own profile
  • Open logs — activity, photographs, expense ledger
  • Progress against each of the five instalments
  • The farmer's own video messages
  • Invitation to the awards, before Durga Puja 2027
  • The farm may carry your bheete's name, or a parent's

Administration and everything around it comes to four per cent — among the lowest anywhere. We would rather print that than bury it. We are 80G registered, so Indian taxpayers can claim the applicable deduction.

The mechanism

The Krishi Ratna League

West Bengal's 294 assembly constituencies are grouped into 15 regional teams — a franchise here is a group of constituencies, not a cricket side. Every farm carries its own marks; a team's score is the aggregate of its farms. There is a table, and it moves. Prizes: ₹1 crore best farm, ₹1 crore best team, ₹1 crore across categories.

The qualification gate

A farm must be free of harmful chemicals and harmful pesticides to be scored at all. The emphasis is on local and herbal solutions — what the soil, the village and our own tradition already know how to make.

And when the farm struggles?

It will. Every farm does. A struggle is not a failure — it is the part of the season where a Pravasi Bandhu becomes useful. The logs stay open precisely so that when a farm is in difficulty you can see it and lend a hand in that battle: a call, a contact, an introduction, an extra push at the right moment. You are not a distant donor reading a report. You are one of the people in the fight.

Who stands behind it

Seven decades, and eighteen years

Bharatiya Krishak Samaj

Founded 3 April 1955 by Dr Panjabrao S. Deshmukh, appointed Agriculture Minister in 1952, who kept the Samaj deliberately outside party politics so it could argue with any government of the day.

National President Shri Krishan Bir Chaudhary sits on the Government of India's Minimum Support Price Committee. Former Chairman of the State Farms Corporation of India and the Indian Sugarcane Development Council; founder of SFAC; former director of NAFED.

Advisory board: Dr R.S. Paroda (ex-DG, ICAR), Siraj Hussain IAS (ex-Secretary, Agriculture), Ashish Bahuguna IAS (ex-Chairman, FSSAI), Dr Baldev Singh Dhillon (ex-VC, PAU), Gopa Sabharwal (ex-VC, Nalanda).

The West Bengal Chapter, established June 2026, is led as State President by MahAcharya Shri Sourabh J. Sarkar, Founder of KarmYog for the 21st Century.

KarmYog for the 21st Century

Founded 2008 by MahAcharya Shri Sourabh J. Sarkar — B.Tech, IIT Kharagpur; M.A. Education & New Media, Syracuse. Its subject has always been the same one: how a population changes a habit.

Kolkata Jaago — Safe Drive Save Life: 300+ schools and roughly one lakh taxi drivers. Jeevan-Jeevika Abhiyaan: changed farming practice in Odisha through the SRI technique.

OmniDEL: NSDC Best Learning Methodology Award 2014; a million-dollar TATA Trusts grant 2015. Now at the forefront of Agentic AI for informal-sector transformation — the capability this League's Support & Verification system runs on.

KarmYog Green Village: ~12 acres of HIDCO wasteland turned into a working eco-village with 2,000+ fruit-bearing trees.
